Privacy

Your troubleshooting data stays focused.

macOS Agent stores the Google account email, account identifiers, credit usage, feedback ratings, and conversation state needed to provide the service. Diagnostic messages are redacted for common personal identifiers before they are sent to model providers.

We do not request Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, or other private Google scopes. Google is used only to authenticate your account. Provider usage totals, error types, and operational logs are used to operate and improve the service.

Do not submit passwords, private keys, financial information, or other sensitive data. To request account deletion or ask a privacy question, contact the project owner through the repository linked on the home page.
